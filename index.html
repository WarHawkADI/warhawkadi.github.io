01 — About

Engineer first, curious always

I'm a Computer Science undergrad at IIIT-Delhi who gravitated toward the unglamorous half of software — the part where data has to arrive on time, stay correct, and explain itself afterwards.

That pull has taken me through three internships: modelling an enterprise data graph and building AWS Glue/Athena pipelines at TBO.com, running a real-time analytics platform on Spring Boot, Kafka and Elasticsearch at LogiCoy, and shipping an ML revenue-forecasting system at HCLTech. The through-line is the same: make the pipeline dependable, then make it fast.

Outside of coursework I'm usually mid-hackathon. Most recently I led my team to first place at the Mastercard Innovation Challenge at Global Fintech Fest 2026 with Adversarial Payment Fraud AI, out of 1,024+ submissions. Drishti and TBO Assemble came from the same habit — projects built under a clock, which turns out to be a good forcing function for deciding what actually matters. I like teams that argue well, ship often, and stay humble about it.

1st · Mastercard GFF 2026 Aug – Sep 2026

Adversarial Payment Fraud AI

Red-teaming AI for GenAI-era payment fraud

A platform that attacks payment systems the way GenAI-era fraudsters would, then learns to stop them. It simulates 67 attack vectors across 9 fraud families and 3 payment data planes, and defends with a stacked detection ensemble — gradient boosting, random forest and isolation forest over behavioural features, backed by security guards. It took first place at the Mastercard Innovation Challenge @ GFF 2026.
